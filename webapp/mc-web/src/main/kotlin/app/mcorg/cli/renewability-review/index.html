MCO-565 · Worth a farm

Renewability Review

Which materials a farm can produce, worked out from the ingested source graph rather than a hand-kept list. An item is renewable when some source makes it from renewable inputs alone. A loop (cobblestone ⇄ stone) proves nothing on its own. Mark each verdict Correct or Wrong; Claude reads the marks back.

Settled items are hidden: their verdict follows from decisions already made. Everything whose verdict matches the committed snapshot is settled, so a version that matches opens with nothing to review, and a new one shows only new items and flipped verdicts. Beyond that, a renewable item settles when one of its proofs is a source kind you accepted and everything it needs is settled renewable. A non-renewable item settles when every source it has is ruled out or needs something settled non-renewable. Amber chips are our own hand-written sources. Accept source on one trusts it everywhere, and each Correct mark can settle the items built on it. Click an ingredient to see its own verdict.

Rules applied (reviewed 7 October 2026)
  • Never proof: chest loot, archaeology, the wandering trader (acquisition, not renewability), Hero of the Village gifts (raid farms no longer stack), elder guardian drops.
  • Proof: mob drops (summoned bosses included), villager trades (trading halls), bartering, fishing, shearing, gameplay drops (eggs, sniffer digging, turtle scutes, cat gifts, armadillo, panda), mob and block interactions, water and lava collection.
  • Breaking a block proves its drops only when the block is itself renewable. The same goes for interacting with a block. A block nothing produces (beetroots, potatoes, cave_vines) is placed by an item, so it counts when any of its drops is renewable. A potted plant needs both the pot and the plant.
  • Recipes prove their output when every input is renewable. A tag input needs any one renewable member.
  • Hand-written mechanics cover what the data cannot see: generators, tree growth (the #logs and #leaves tags), huge fungi and mushrooms, chorus, freezing, amethyst, sculk, vines, bone meal on grass and nylium, the bone-meal flowers (listed by hand; the ingest keeps no flower tag), copper oxidation, anvil wear, copper golems turning into statues, sniffer and turtle breeding, withers making wither roses, torchflowers growing, and the chain stone → moss → azalea → rooted dirt → dirt → mud → clay.
  • Not a material: the nether portal is lit in place, like air or the end portal, and is left out of the list.
  • A version with no ingested trades (every 1.x version) borrows the villager trades of the newest version that has them, kept only where every item exists in the older version.
  • Not modelled yet: dupers (sand, concrete, TNT), which wait on a world saying it accepts them (MCO-484), and idea-bank designs as proof.
